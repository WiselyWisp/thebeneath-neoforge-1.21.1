The Beneath is a mysterious and unsettling dimension mod for Minecraft, where darkness hides forgotten ruins, strange creatures, and ancient secrets.

Explore the eerie landscape beneath the void and hope you don't encounter mysterious entities.

The Beneath offers an immersive exploration experience where the unknown is always lurking.

Enter The Beneath. Explore the forgotten. Fear the unknown.

| Dependency  | Requirement |
